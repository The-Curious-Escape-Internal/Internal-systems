Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize access through Discord OAuth2, we retrieve limited account metadata required for app functionality:

  • Discord Username and Profile Discriminator
  • Discord User ID
  • Avatar Hash (used strictly for displaying profile pictures)
  • Channel message data necessary for chat rendering

2. How Data is Used

Collected data is used solely to render your chat session inside the app interface. We do not sell, store on third-party servers, or share your personal profile data.

3. Data Storage & Security

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device session and are used exclusively to authenticate API requests directly with Discord.

4. Revoking Access

You can revoke access at any time through your Discord account settings under Authorized Apps.
